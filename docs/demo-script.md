# Gem Finder — two-minute demo script

Target: approximately two minutes, including brief pauses for screen actions. Use a Reel that has completed a real test run, with an explicit target and deadline. Keep the displayed conclusion and numbers from that run. Demonstrate the iPhone handoff on the actual device before recording; shorten waiting time with a clearly labeled edit.

## Jake · 0:00–0:18

*Screen: a financial Reel making a confident Bitcoin prediction, then the share button.*

“You’ve seen this Reel: ‘Buy Bitcoin. It’s going to 150K. Guaranteed.’

But how do you know whether that confidence is backed by evidence?

We built Gem Finder to help you check the claim before putting money behind it.”

## Rayan · 0:18–0:55

*Screen: Instagram → Share → More / Share to… → Check with Gem Finder. Show the transcript and automatic report. Use the exact target and deadline from the tested Reel.*

“See a prediction? Open the Share menu and choose Check with Gem Finder.

It transcribes the Reel, identifies up to three claims, and automatically searches for relevant Gemini prediction markets.

For a supported Bitcoin price target, our model also uses Gemini price history to calculate estimates across three volatility windows.

It separates touching a target from finishing above it. And when a Gemini contract has a different target or deadline, we show that difference. Those aren’t interchangeable odds.”

## Elijah · 0:55–1:25

*Screen: the report’s actual conclusion, followed by Market overview → Quotes → Timeline. Then open Review trade and stop at the private login or reviewed order; do not imply an order has executed.*

“Here’s the result: a clear conclusion, with the evidence and its limits visible.

Want more context? Explore related Gemini contracts, compare quotes and deadlines, and revisit your saved analyses.

If you decide to trade, our connected owner demo lets you review a Gemini order, with a five-dollar total limit and manual confirmation.

Checking a Reel never places a trade automatically.”

## Jake · 1:25–2:00

*Screen: the Reel beside its related Gemini markets. Finish on the Gem Finder name, team names, and live app link.*

“Our conversations with Gemini at this hackathon highlighted an opportunity: helping more people discover and understand prediction markets.

Gem Finder brings Gemini into something people already do every day—scrolling. A prediction in your feed becomes a reason to explore a market and understand what it actually prices.

Next time someone says a coin is about to blow up: check the confidence, understand the odds, then decide.

Send it to Gem Finder. It’s live now.”

## Recording notes

- The automatic report has a plain-language conclusion. Show a large HYPE verdict only if you deliberately switch to the separate single-crypto checker and that check produces it.
- Do not say a market verifies a fact or that both estimates are below a number unless the displayed contract matches the claim’s event, target, deadline, and outcome definition.
- The model is an independent calculation using Gemini data, not a second independent data source.
- Saved analyses are browser-tab snapshots, not live quotes or a cloud account history.
- The trading demo is for the configured owner account. Order preview and manual submission are separate; no real order has been verified in this project’s recorded checks.
- Do not display API keys, owner access codes, or account identifiers in the video.
