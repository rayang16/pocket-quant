Add Check with Pocket Quant to your iPhone’s Share Sheet using Apple Shortcuts.

Get the iPhone shortcut ↓
  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. Download the shortcut, then open the downloaded file in Shortcuts and tap Add Shortcut. If it opens in Files, tap the file there.
  2. In Shortcuts, open Check with Pocket Quant, open its Details, and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
  3. On an Instagram Reel, tap Share, then More or Share to… to open the iPhone Share Sheet.
  4. Scroll down to the action list and tap Check with Pocket Quant. Allow opening the Pocket Quant website if iOS asks.
  5. Pocket Quant gets the public Reel’s transcript, finds up to three claims, and automatically checks Gemini markets and any applicable crypto math. Your results fill in without another tap. You can edit a claim and recheck it, or stop waiting and paste the words instead.

Instagram shares a link. When enabled, Pocket Quant sends that public link to Supadata to request a transcript. Private, unavailable, or unsupported Reels may not work; paste the spoken claim or upload a recording instead. Reel and recording transcripts are checked with Gemini automatically. For pasted words, tap Check with Gemini once.

Can’t see the shortcut?

In Shortcuts, open Check with Pocket Quant, open its details, and enable Show in Share Sheet. If Instagram doesn’t offer the system Share Sheet, copy the Reel link, return to Pocket Quant, and use Add a Reel link.

What gets shared?

The shortcut URL-encodes the shared content and opens our public checker. The app removes Instagram tracking parameters before requesting a transcript. Public Reel links are sent to Supadata when automatic transcription is enabled; uploaded recordings are sent to ElevenLabs when recording transcription is enabled. Pocket Quant does not log into Instagram, access private videos, or keep a saved Reel history. Gemini checks use public prediction market data and, for supported crypto targets, public prices and price history. Checking a Reel does not place a trade.

Keep Pocket Quant nearby

In Safari, open the checker, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Share Sheet shortcut remains the way to send a Reel into the app.
