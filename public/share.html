Add Check with Pocket Quant to your iPhone’s Share Sheet using Apple Shortcuts.

Get the iPhone shortcut ↓
  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. Download the shortcut, then open the downloaded file in Shortcuts and tap Add Shortcut. If it opens in Files, tap the file there.
  2. On an Instagram Reel, tap Share, then More or Share to… to open the iPhone Share Sheet.
  3. Scroll down to Check with Pocket Quant. Tap it to open the Reel in Pocket Quant. Allow opening the Pocket Quant website if iOS asks.
  4. For a public Reel, Pocket Quant tries to get its transcript automatically when enabled. Review the words, keep one prediction, then tap Check the hype. You can stop waiting and paste the claim, or upload a screen recording when recording transcription is enabled.

Instagram shares a link. When enabled, Pocket Quant sends that public link to Supadata to request a transcript. Private, unavailable, or unsupported Reels may not work; paste the spoken claim or upload a recording instead. A transcript is never automatically analyzed: you review it and choose Check the hype.

Can’t see the shortcut?

In Shortcuts, open Check with Pocket Quant, open its details, and enable Show in Share Sheet. If Instagram doesn’t offer the system Share Sheet, copy the Reel link, return to Pocket Quant, and use Add a Reel link.

What gets shared?

The shortcut URL-encodes the shared content and opens our public checker. The app removes Instagram tracking parameters before requesting a transcript. Public Reel links are sent to Supadata when automatic transcription is enabled; uploaded recordings are sent to ElevenLabs when recording transcription is enabled. Pocket Quant does not log into Instagram, access private videos, or keep a saved Reel history. Predictions are sent for checking only when you choose Check the hype.

Keep Pocket Quant nearby

In Safari, open the checker, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Share Sheet shortcut remains the way to send a Reel into the app.
